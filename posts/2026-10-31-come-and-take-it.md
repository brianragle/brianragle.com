Sam Altman went on the Cheeky Pint podcast and said the quiet part in full sentences. From a global allocation perspective, he argued, it is "quite terrible" to have everybody holding a share of the world's limited DRAM supply in their own computers, used 5% of the time. Maximum utilization lives in the cloud. His cloud, which you would rent back from him one task at a time.

Gizmodo has the story: [Sam Altman: All the RAM Should Belong to Me, So I Can Charge People to Use It](https://gizmodo.com/sam-altman-all-the-ram-should-belong-to-me-so-i-can-charge-people-to-use-it-2000833412)

He can have my RAM the way Santa Anna could have the cannon at Gonzales. Two memes, made this morning. The flag first flew in the cartoon; here it is on its own.
