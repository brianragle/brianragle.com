The Community Action Coalition of Cookeville held its second Really Really Free Market on Saturday, Sept. 26, at Meadows Pavilion in Cane Creek Park. Everything was free, no cash box, no price tags, no sign-up sheet. I shot video alongside the Herald-Citizen follow-up reporting. Here is the set.
