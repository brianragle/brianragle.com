Privacy Policy — Desert Honey Uploader

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Desert Honey Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by Brian Ragle (brianragle.com). It is not a public service and has no other users.

What the app does

The app uploads videos, thumbnails, captions, titles, descriptions, and tags to Brian Ragle’s own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API v3, under Brian Ragle’s own Google account authorization (OAuth 2.0).

Data accessed

The app accesses only the YouTube account data needed to perform those uploads on the account owner’s behalf. The OAuth refresh token is stored locally, on Brian Ragle’s own computer, in the app’s private configuration folder. It is not transmitted to any third party, sold, shared, or used for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data collection and retention

The app collects no personal information about any other person. No data is stored on any server operated by the app. Upload logs are kept locally on the operator’s computer for operational purposes only.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account’s third-party access page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately invalidates the stored token.
